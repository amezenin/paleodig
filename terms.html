7. Educational and Scientific Content

PaleoDig includes educational information about prehistoric organisms, geological periods, human evolution, fossils, archaeology, ancient technologies and early civilisations.

We aim to present scientifically informed and reasonably accurate information based on available scientific research and archaeological knowledge.

Scientific understanding may change as new discoveries, dating methods and research become available.

Some archaeological and palaeontological interpretations are uncertain or debated. Proposed behaviours, reconstructed environments and historical explanations may change with new evidence.

Educational texts and translations may contain inaccuracies, omissions or terminology that requires revision. We may correct or update such material in future releases.

Images, illustrations and prehistoric reconstructions may include AI-generated artistic interpretations of extinct organisms, ancient people, archaeological artifacts, historical locations and environments.

Such illustrations are intended for educational visualisation and entertainment. They should not be treated as original archaeological photographs, exact scientific reproductions or definitive evidence of the appearance of ancient organisms, objects or events.

The Game may contain non-sexual depictions of prehistoric people, historically appropriate clothing, archaeological human and animal remains, and scientific references to hunting, injuries and death.

These materials are presented in an educational and historical context.

Educational material is provided for general information and entertainment and is not intended to replace professional scientific research or formal education.
