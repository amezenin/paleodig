PaleoDig — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PaleoDig (the "Game"), published by Mezhenon Production (the "Developer", "we", "us"), handles information when you play the Game on Android.

Adults only (18+).

The Game is intended exclusively for users aged 18 or older. It is not directed to children or minors. The Game includes optional rewarded advertisements and may offer purchases using real money.

Players are not required to watch advertisements or make purchases to complete the main game. There are no mandatory advertisements between levels.

If you are under 18, please do not use the Game or make purchases.

1. Information handled by the Game

PaleoDig is designed to work without creating an account or providing your real name.

The Game may save the following information locally on your device:

  • Completed levels and gameplay progress.
  • Unlocked prehistoric creatures and discoveries.
  • Research and puzzle completion status.
  • Virtual coin balances and reward history.
  • Language preferences and game settings.

These records are used to maintain your progress and preferences between sessions.

Third-party services integrated into the Game may automatically process information such as your device's advertising identifier (when available and permitted), IP address, approximate location inferred from the IP address, device and app information, advertising interactions, purchase information, and technical diagnostics.

The Game does not require precise GPS location to function and does not intentionally request precise location permission.

2. Optional rewarded advertising

PaleoDig uses Google AdMob to provide optional rewarded advertisements.

Players may voluntarily watch an advertisement to receive gameplay assistance, including:

  • Hints for puzzle levels.
  • Scanning hidden fossil locations.
  • Rescuing progress after a mistake.
  • Additional undo actions in sorting puzzles.

Watching advertisements is optional. Gameplay assistance may also be available through virtual coins earned in the Game or obtained through optional purchases.

There are no mandatory advertisements between levels. Declining an advertisement does not prevent normal gameplay.

Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising performance data and related information to deliver, measure, secure and, where permitted, personalize advertising.

Depending on your region and privacy choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

For more information, see Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information .

3. Advertising consent and privacy choices

Where legally required, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present applicable advertising privacy choices.

Your choices may affect advertising personalization and how advertising-related information is processed.

Where required, the Game provides a way to revisit available advertising privacy choices.

You may also use your Android device's privacy settings to manage or reset its advertising ID, where supported.

Withdrawing consent does not necessarily prevent all processing permitted or required under other applicable legal grounds.

4. In-app purchases and virtual currency

PaleoDig may offer optional purchases of virtual coins through Google Play Billing.

Virtual coins can be used to obtain optional gameplay assistance, such as hints, scans, rescues and additional undo actions.

Payments are processed by Google Play. The Developer does not receive or store your full payment card number or payment credentials.

Google Play may provide limited transaction information, including purchase status, product identifiers and transaction tokens, to support purchase verification, delivery, fraud prevention and transaction recovery.

Purchases are optional. Prices and payment confirmations are displayed through Google Play. Refunds are subject to applicable law and Google Play policies.

For further information, see the Google Privacy Policy .

5. Why information is used

  • To operate the Game and save progress, discoveries and settings.
  • To provide optional rewarded advertisements and grant gameplay assistance after eligible ad completions.
  • To process and validate optional purchases and deliver purchased virtual currency.
  • To maintain security, detect fraud and resolve technical problems.
  • To comply with applicable legal requirements and respect privacy choices.

6. Third-party services

The Game uses or may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP).
  • Google Play Billing.

These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and may transfer data internationally, subject to applicable legal safeguards.

We do not sell player contact lists or directly operate a marketplace for personal information. Advertising providers may process or share certain data as described in their policies and consent notices.

7. Data retention, deletion and security

Local gameplay records and preferences generally remain on your device until you clear the Game's app data or uninstall it, subject to Android backup behavior and platform settings.

Clearing local data may permanently remove saved progress, unlocked discoveries, virtual currency records and preferences.

Google and other integrated providers manage retention periods for information they process under their own policies.

You may contact the Developer regarding privacy requests relating to information under our control.

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no digital system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict certain processing of your personal information, object to processing, or withdraw consent.

You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with an applicable data-protection authority.

For information processed by Google, use Google's privacy controls or contact Google directly.

For Developer-related inquiries, contact us using the email address below.

9. Children's privacy

PaleoDig is intended for adults aged 18 and older. It is not designed for, marketed to, or directed at children.

We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children.

If you believe a minor has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us so we can evaluate and address the issue.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game's features, service providers or legal requirements change.

Updated versions will be published at the same public URL with a revised effective date.

Material changes may require additional notice or consent where applicable.
