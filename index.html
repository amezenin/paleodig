PaleoDig — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PaleoDig (the "Game"), published by Mezhenon Production (the "Developer", "we", "us"), handles information when you use the Game on Android.

Adults only (18+).

The Game is intended for users aged 18 or older and is not directed to children.

PaleoDig does not display advertisements. The Game contains a free campaign and offers optional in-app purchases, including virtual Coin packages and access to additional campaigns. The free campaign does not require a purchase.

If you are under 18, please do not use the Game or make purchases.

1. Information stored on your device

You can play PaleoDig without creating an in-game account or providing your real name.

The Game stores or may store information locally on your device, including:

  • Completed levels, unlocked levels and game progress.
  • Unlocked prehistoric creatures, archaeological discoveries and collection entries.
  • Research and puzzle completion status.
  • Virtual Coin balances, earned rewards and available assistance.
  • Purchased-content entitlement status, language preferences and game settings.

We use these local records to provide gameplay features, remember your progress and restore your in-game preferences between sessions. Locally stored information is not automatically sent to the Developer merely because it is stored on your device.

The Game does not require precise GPS location to function and does not intentionally request precise-location permission.

2. No advertising or advertising consent

PaleoDig does not contain rewarded advertisements, banner advertisements or interstitial advertisements. The ad-free version does not integrate Google AdMob or Google's advertising User Messaging Platform (UMP).

The Game does not request an advertising identifier for advertising purposes, use an advertising consent dialog or give rewards in exchange for watching advertisements.

Optional gameplay assistance, where available, is obtained through the Game's virtual Coin system. Coins can be earned through gameplay or purchased through Google Play.

3. Optional in-app purchases

PaleoDig offers optional purchases through Google Play Billing, which may include:

  • Packages of virtual Coins for eligible in-game assistance, such as hints, scans, rescues and undo actions.
  • Unlocking the additional Human Origins campaign.
  • Unlocking the additional Traces of Humanity campaign.

The History of Life campaign is available without purchasing an additional campaign. Prices, availability and eligible promotions or discounts are shown through Google Play before you approve payment.

Google Play handles payment processing. We do not receive your full payment-card number or payment credentials. The Game may receive and process limited purchase-related data, such as product identifiers, purchase status and purchase tokens, to grant purchased content, acknowledge transactions, prevent duplicate fulfilment and restore eligible purchases.

Purchase information is handled in accordance with the Google Play services and policies applicable to the transaction. For details, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service .

Virtual Coins have no cash value outside the Game and cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for money through PaleoDig. Purchases and refunds are subject to applicable consumer protection laws and Google Play rules.

4. Information processed by Google and other platform services

When you download, update or purchase content in the Game through Google Play, Google may process device and app information, IP address, account and payment-related details, purchase records and technical diagnostics as part of its own platform services. Google processes this information under its own privacy policies.

PaleoDig does not require you to provide an in-game profile or a contact list. We do not use advertising SDKs to track gameplay or build advertising profiles.

Depending on your Android settings, Google or your device manufacturer may also operate system-level functions such as backups, security checks or crash reporting. These platform functions are not equivalent to an advertising SDK integrated into the Game.

5. Why information is used

  • To operate the Game, maintain puzzle progress, collections and research unlocks.
  • To manage virtual Coins, earned rewards and optional gameplay assistance.
  • To process and restore purchases of Coin packages and additional campaigns.
  • To validate purchase status, prevent duplicate fulfilment and address purchase problems.
  • To maintain technical functionality, respond to support inquiries and comply with applicable law.

The Game does not use information to serve or personalise in-app advertisements.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local gameplay data generally remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the Game, subject to Android backup and device settings. Clearing data or uninstalling may remove local progress, game settings and earned or purchased virtual Coin balances stored only on the device.

Eligibility to restore previously purchased non-consumable campaigns may be determined through Google Play purchase records. The ability to recover virtual Coin balances or other local progress is not guaranteed.

Google controls the retention of information processed by its services, including Google Play transactions, under its own applicable policies.

To ask about information under the Developer's control, contact the address below. We will respond to relevant requests in accordance with applicable law.

7. Security and international processing

We take reasonable steps to limit the information needed by the Game and to protect the information we control. However, no device or digital service can guarantee complete security.

Google Play and other Android platform services may process data in countries outside your country of residence in accordance with their applicable privacy terms and legal safeguards.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information held by the Developer, and to object to certain processing. In the European Economic Area, you may also have the right to lodge a complaint with the relevant data-protection authority.

For information held by Google, please use Google's privacy controls or contact Google. For data stored only on your device, you can use Android's app-storage settings, bearing in mind that clearing app data may delete game progress.

For questions or requests concerning information within our control, contact us using the email address below.

9. Children's privacy

The Game is intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not intentionally design or market it for children or solicit children's personal information.

If you believe that a minor has provided personal information directly to the Developer, please contact us so we can assess and address the request.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the Game, its purchase features, the services it uses or applicable legal requirements.

The updated policy will be published at this URL with a revised effective date. Where required by applicable law, we will provide additional notice or request consent.
